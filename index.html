About

Before I supported customers, I was counting stock.

Selling taught me to listen. Support taught me to stay.

I started in inventory, where a number is either right or it costs someone money. Counting, auditing, reconciling and chasing suppliers taught me to care about accuracy before I'd heard of automation.

Administration gave me structure, and a team to lead. Sales taught me to ask what a customer needs before offering anything, and to follow up even when it feels awkward. Customer support taught me patience, plain communication and ownership: you stay with a problem until it's closed.

I didn't plan those as separate careers. They turned out to be one skill set, seen from different sides of the same business. Now they work together.
